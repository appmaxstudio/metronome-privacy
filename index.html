Privacy Policy

Metronome — Android application

Last updated: November 26, 2026

This Privacy Policy explains how the Metronome Android application ("the App") handles information. Metronome is a free metronome app for musicians. It does not require account creation, does not offer subscriptions, and does not sell anything inside the app.

Information We Do Not Collect

Metronome does not require you to create an account, and does not collect or store your name, email address, contacts, photos, location, or any other personal information on our own servers. We have no servers: the App has no backend of its own.

Local Preferences

The App saves your settings (tempo, time signature, subdivision, accents, sound, volume, theme) locally on your device only, using Android's DataStore. This data never leaves your device and is not accessible to us.

Advertising

Metronome is funded by advertising shown through Google AdMob (banner and interstitial ads). To serve and measure ads, Google and its advertising partners may collect and process information such as your advertising ID, device information, and general usage data, in accordance with their own privacy policies.

  • Google Privacy Policy
  • How Google uses information from sites or apps that use its services

Where required by applicable law (for example in the European Economic Area, the UK, and certain U.S. states), the App uses Google's User Messaging Platform (UMP) to request your consent, or to present privacy choices, before loading personalized ads. You can review or change your choice at any time from the App's Settings screen ("Privacy Options").

Third-Party Services

The App uses the following third-party services, each governed by its own privacy policy:

  • Google AdMob (advertising)
  • Google User Messaging Platform (consent management)
  • Google Play services

The App does not integrate Firebase, analytics SDKs, or any other third-party service beyond those listed above.

Children's Privacy

The App is not directed at children under the age of 13, and we do not knowingly collect personal information from anyone under that age. If we become aware that a child under 13 has provided us with personal information, we will take steps to delete it. Ad requests are served in accordance with Google AdMob's policies regarding child-directed treatment where applicable.

Data Retention and Deletion

Since all app preferences are stored locally on your device, you can delete all data associated with the App at any time by uninstalling it, or by clearing the App's storage from your device's system settings.

Changes to This Policy

We may update this Privacy Policy from time to time. Changes will be posted on this page with an updated "Last updated" date.
